Nuestra historia

From Tampico to Houston

Tacos La Bala started as a single taco truck in Tampico in 1973. Five decades later we're still a family business, still making every plate to order, and still serving the trompo Houston talks about.

  1. 1973Our first taco truck opens in Tampico, Mexico.
  2. HoustonThe family brings the recipes north and starts with a taco trailer.
  3. Today8 Houston-area taquerías, still family-owned.
